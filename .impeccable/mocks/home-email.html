Works with your Gmail or Outlook mailbox

Each person on the team can connect their own Google or Microsoft mailbox, and email with customers from the same place as their text messages and calls.

Send from your own address

Emails you write to a lead or a contact in the CRM go out through the mailbox you connect, so the customer sees your address and answers you directly.

Replies are filed on the contact

Every1Connects reads your inbox to find the messages that come from your leads and contacts and adds them to that person’s history. Mail that is not part of a conversation with one of them is not stored.

You stay in control

You can disconnect your mailbox at any time from the settings of the CRM. Disconnecting withdraws the access you gave and deletes the access keys we held.

How we use the data of your Google account

Every1Connects uses the access you give to your Gmail mailbox for two things only: to send the emails you write in the CRM, and to show you the emails your leads and contacts send you. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

Every1Connects’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The details are in our Privacy Policy.
