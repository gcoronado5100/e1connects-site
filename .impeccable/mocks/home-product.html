What your team does in Every1Connects

A dealership’s sales work, from the first enquiry to the sale, without moving between tools.

  1. 01

    Leads from your website

    Forms filled in on your dealership’s website arrive in the CRM as they are sent, already matched to the right person. A list of leads can also be imported from a file.

  2. 02

    Chloe, the AI assistant

    Chloe answers new leads by text message, collects what the sales team needs to know and hands the conversation to a person when the customer asks for one.

  3. 03

    One inbox for every channel

    Text messages, calls and emails sit side by side. Anyone on the team can see where a conversation stands, put it aside for later or mark it as done.

  4. 04

    A phone inside the CRM

    Make and take calls from the browser, record them, transfer them to a colleague and work through a list of people with the dialer.

  5. 05

    Contacts and sales pipeline

    Each person has one record with everything that was said and done. Deals move through the stages your dealership works with, and the lists your team opens every day can be saved.

  6. 06

    Tasks and activity reports

    Reminders keep follow-ups from being missed, and reports show what each person on the team did during the day.

Available in English, French and Spanish.
