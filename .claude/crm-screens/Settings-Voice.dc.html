Batch and one-by-one dialing from the Inbox, Leads and Smart Views.

Allow the dialer outside CRTC calling hours

Off: the dialer only calls 9:00–21:30 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on weekends. Manual calls are never restricted.

Canada's telemarketing rules (CRTC) only allow sales calls from 9:00 am to 9:30 pm on weekdays and from 10:00 am to 6:00 pm on weekends, in the customer's local time. They apply to calls made by your agents, not only automated ones, and also to customers who recently enquired or bought from you (that only exempts them from the National Do Not Call List). Turning this on is your organization's compliance decision. Calls that are not sales (returning a customer's call, an application in progress, an appointment) are not affected. An AI voice agent will always keep these hours.
