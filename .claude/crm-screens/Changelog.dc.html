Changelog

Complete history of changes and fixes per version.

v1.3.1October 2nd, 2026

Changes

  • Phone panel: click the phone icon in the header to turn "Receive calls" on or off (the switch moved here from the header), choose your caller ID, turn auto-recording on or off, pick your audio devices and dial any number. A green dot on the icon means you are taking calls; an amber dot means something needs your attention, such as blocked notifications or microphone.
  • Dial any number: type or paste it in any format, for example (506) 555-0100. While you type, the panel shows which contact or lead the number belongs to, and invalid numbers are flagged before you call.
  • Call card: the caller's name now opens their profile on every call, and the card shows the contact's status next to the name.
  • Wrong number replies close themselves: when a customer says you have the wrong number, that number is opted out, the chat moves to Done and the pipeline card moves to Wrong Number, instead of waiting in Needs Agent.
  • Faster Chloe replies after bulk sends: Chloe now answers two customers at a time, so a large send no longer leaves customers waiting several minutes for a reply.

Fixes

  • Incoming calls no longer vanish when the CRM is open in several tabs or windows. A tab stuck on an earlier call could turn new calls away and keep the ringtone playing.
  • Batch dialer: the monthly abandoned-call limit now counts every call the dialer makes, as the CRTC rule does, so a few abandoned calls early in the month no longer pause batch dialing until the next month.
  • Chloe can now move older imported pipeline cards that have no stage into Lead In when the customer qualifies.
  • A call to a typed number is now logged on the right contact even when the contact's phone was saved in a different format.
v1.3.0September 24th, 2026

Changes

  • Phone calls in the CRM: call customers from the browser softphone, answer inbound calls on the dealership's lines, transfer a call to a teammate and record calls. Every call is logged on the contact's timeline and in the new Calls tab of the Inbox.
  • AI call notes: after a recorded call, the CRM transcribes it, adds a summary note to the contact and suggests the customer details it heard, so you can apply them to the profile in one click.
  • Power dialer: work through a list or a Smart View one call at a time or several lines at once. Batch mode keeps the other lines ringing while it checks whether a pick-up is a person or a voicemail, and each person is called only once even if they sent several forms.
  • Smarter inbound routing: incoming calls ring the agents who are online first, and agents who are away ring last. Admins can make the softphone available automatically as soon as agents open the app (Settings → Organization); Do not disturb still turns it off.
  • Activity reports (Reports menu): Activity Overview, Activity Comparison per user, Calls, Status Changes, Explorer, Sales Funnel and the Activity Log, with date ranges, period comparison, user and Smart View filters, saved reports and CSV export. Agents see their own numbers; admins see the whole team. Online / away / offline status now shows in the header, on the dashboard and in the members list.
  • New filters: Leads, Contacts, Inbox, Opportunities and Calls share one filter panel (is, is not, is any of, is blank, is within…). New fields include SMS sent and received, chat status, phone unreachable, source website and "has no opportunity".
  • Smart Views for all leads: a Smart View can now list every lead that matches its filters, not only leads that already have a pipeline card. Create one from the Leads page with "Save as".
  • Text from another line or to another number: in a contact's chat, pick the dealership line to send from and the contact's number to send to, as a manual message or with Chloe. Chloe's follow-ups on the contact's other lines pause, and one new line per contact can be opened every 24 hours to protect the numbers from spam labels. You can also set your own default sending line.
  • Chat language and translation: each chat has a language (English, French or Spanish) that Chloe follows, and any chat can be translated into your language with one click. Translation is display only; nothing extra is sent to the customer.
  • Scheduled sends: write a message or start Chloe now and have it go out at a chosen date and time.
  • One record per person: every form submission is linked to the customer's contact, duplicate contacts can be merged, and the contact timeline shows who changed which details. Notes and pipeline cards can be pinned.
  • Pipeline stage settings: mark stages as Won or Lost, stop Chloe's follow-ups for cards in a stage or restart them when a card enters it, and require a complete credit application before Chloe moves a card into a stage.
  • Sign-in: choose "Remember this device for 3 days", your session renews while you work, and you can review the devices signed in to your account.
  • Lower SMS costs: Chloe's messages now use plain punctuation, so each one takes roughly a third fewer SMS segments.

Fixes

  • Leads: "Opportunity stage is blank" no longer lists leads whose contact already has a pipeline card. The Opportunity column, the Stage filter and exports now show the contact's card on every lead of that person.
  • Batch dialer: a voicemail pick-up no longer hangs up the other ringing lines, and a line hung up because someone else answered keeps its call attempt.
  • CSV imports no longer relabel existing website leads as "csv_import".
  • Smart Views filtered by SMS sent, SMS received or chat status now include cards linked only to a contact.
  • A text from a contact's second phone number now lands on that contact instead of creating an unlinked chat.
v1.2.2May 13th, 2026

Changes

  • Task Drawer: tasks now open in a side drawer for view, edit, reassign, and delete — replacing the create-only modal. Click any task row in the Inbox tasks tab or in the client/lead Tasks panel to open it.
  • Edit & delete tasks: admins, masters, owners, and the task creator can now edit any field (title, type, priority, due date, notes, assignee) or delete a task with confirmation. Read-only view for everyone else.
  • Email reminders for tasks: when a pending task is 5–15 minutes from its due time and the assignee has been inactive in the app for 5+ minutes, they receive a heads-up email with a one-click link to the task. Idempotent — no duplicate sends.
  • User activity tracking: every authenticated request updates the user's last-seen timestamp (throttled to once per minute). Powers the inactivity check for email reminders and lays groundwork for future presence features.
  • Inbox bulk Take: select multiple chats in the Chloe or Needs Agent tabs and take control of them all in one click. Chats already assigned to another agent are skipped automatically and reported.
  • Phone unreachable visibility: when a customer has opted out (STOP) or their number is unreachable, the inbox table shows a red phone-off icon next to their name; the chat view shows an explicit banner explaining the reason; and the message composer is disabled to prevent failed sends.

Fixes

  • Critical — Task blocking modal stuck on screen: the modal was appearing in every page from the moment a task was created until its due time, instead of only at the due time. Backend now filters tasks by due_at <= now() before returning them to the modal poller.
  • Critical — Tasks created 'for later today' appearing as overdue: the create-task form was sending the due date without a timezone marker, so Laravel (UTC) interpreted the local time as UTC and the task came back already past due. The form now converts local time to ISO 8601 UTC before sending.
  • Security — Task update endpoint had no permission check: any user in the organization could edit any task. Now restricted to the task creator or an admin/master/owner, matching the existing delete behavior.
  • Sending an SMS template to an opted-out customer returned a 422 with no in-app indication: the chat now blocks the send up front with an explanation, and the customer's status is visible across the Inbox list, the chat banner, and the client panel.
